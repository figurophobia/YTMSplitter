# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want YTMSplitter to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
experience, identity, background or language. Be kind, assume good faith and
criticise ideas rather than people.

## Expected behaviour

- Use respectful and inclusive language.
- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Respect differing opinions and use cases.
- Credit other people's work.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Posting credentials, cookies or personal music data.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads or block contributors
who violate this code. To report an incident, email **dasamiarea@gmail.com**.
Reports will be handled privately and in good faith.

This code is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
